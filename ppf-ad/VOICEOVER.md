## Revising the wording

Changes are welcome before generation. Narration that differs from the
on-screen text breaks the "read what is on screen" rule, so for each change
either the on-screen text changes too (a small re-render of that shot), or
the line is marked as voice-only. Any line that grows longer may need a
slightly longer hold on its shot; the score re-times automatically.

## Production plan once the plan is active

1. Two takes per section (S1 lines 1–5 · S2 lines 6–9 · S3 lines 10–13 ·
   S4 lines 14–18) with Mohammed.
2. Transcribe every take and check it word for word against this script.
3. Cut into lines, place at the slots above (`vo` in `src/timeline.json`).
4. Vocal chain: 80 Hz high-pass, de-ess, gentle 3:1 compression, light
   presence lift, short subtle reverb, limiter. Music ducks 6–8 dB under the
   voice (`scripts/mix-vo.sh`). Final −14 LUFS, true peak below −2 dBTP.
5. Audio-only mix and a preview video for REK's review; final export only
   after sign-off.
